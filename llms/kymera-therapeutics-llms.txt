# Kymera Therapeutics

> Clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Watertown, Massachusetts building oral
> small-molecule medicines with targeted protein degradation — degraders that recruit
> the body's own protein-disposal machinery to eliminate disease-driving proteins that
> conventional inhibitors cannot drug. Kymera publishes no public developer API,
> developer portal, SDK, or machine-readable API description; this file describes the
> company's public web surface for agents and research tools.

## Pipeline programs

- KT-621 — oral STAT6 degrader, Phase 2, wholly owned. Type 2 inflammatory disease: atopic dermatitis, asthma, COPD, eosinophilic esophagitis, chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps, chronic spontaneous urticaria, prurigo nodularis, bullous pemphigoid.
- KT-579 — first-in-class oral IRF5 degrader, Phase 1, wholly owned. Lupus, Sjogren's syndrome, rheumatoid arthritis, inflammatory bowel disease, systemic sclerosis, dermatomyositis.
- KT-485 / SAR447971 — oral IRAK4 degrader, Phase 1, partnered with Sanofi. Hidradenitis suppurativa, atopic dermatitis, rheumatoid arthritis, asthma, IBD.
- KT-200 — CDK2 molecular glue degrader, IND-enabling, partnered with Gilead Sciences. CCNE1-amplified breast cancer and solid tumors.
